## 3. PHYSICS AND CHEMISTRY CORRECTIONS

### 3.1 SPACE GROUP OF THE POLAR PHASE
- **OLD:** "P4/MMM IS THE CONVENTIONAL HIGH TEMPERATURE TETRAGONAL GROUP."
- **WRONG BECAUSE:** P4/MMM IS CENTROSYMMETRIC AND THEREFORE CANNOT SUPPORT A
  SPONTANEOUS POLARISATION, AND IT IS NOT THE HIGH TEMPERATURE PHASE EITHER.
- **NEW:** ABOVE THE CURIE TEMPERATURE OF 393 K THE STRUCTURE IS CUBIC PM-3M.
  BELOW IT THE POLAR PHASE IS P4MM, NUMBER 99. P4/MMM APPEARS ONLY AS THE
  NON POLAR REFERENCE CELL AT THE FIXED AXIAL RATIO 1.0282.

### 3.2 REMANENCE AND COERCIVITY VERSUS THE Z PARAMETER
- **OLD:** THE SCRIPT ASSERTED THAT Z = 0.510 "IS" THE REMANENT STATE AND
  Z = 0.500 "IS" THE COERCIVE STATE, AS IF THESE WERE COMPUTED RESULTS.
- **WRONG BECAUSE:** THE Z SCAN IS A RIGID DISPLACEMENT MODEL WITH NO DOMAIN
  WALLS, NO DEFECTS AND NO TEMPERATURE, SO IT CANNOT PREDICT EITHER QUANTITY.
- **NEW:** THE MAPPING IS PRESENTED AS THE LABELLING SUPPLIED IN THE BRIEF, AND
  AN EXPLICIT CAVEAT STATES THAT A SINGLE CELL CALCULATION GIVES THE HOMOGENEOUS
  SWITCHING FIELD, NEAR 1E8 VOLTS PER METRE, WHEREAS REAL COERCIVE FIELDS ARE
  1E5 TO 1E6 VOLTS PER METRE BECAUSE SWITCHING PROCEEDS BY DOMAIN WALL MOTION.

### 3.3 CATE LATTICE EXPANSION
- **OLD:** THE EXPANSION FROM NACL TO CATE WAS ATTRIBUTED TO THE LARGER CATION.
- **WRONG BECAUSE:** CALCIUM AT 100 PICOMETRES IS ACTUALLY SMALLER THAN SODIUM
  AT 102 PICOMETRES AT COORDINATION NUMBER 6.
- **NEW:** THE EXPANSION IS ANION DRIVEN. TELLURIDE AT 221 PICOMETRES AGAINST
  CHLORIDE AT 181 PICOMETRES CARRIES THE WHOLE 71.6 PICOMETRE INCREASE IN THE
  LATTICE PARAMETER.

### 3.4 GALLIUM PHOSPHIDE
- **OLD:** "DIRECT AND INDIRECT TRANSITIONS."
- **WRONG BECAUSE:** GAP IS AN INDIRECT GAP SEMICONDUCTOR; THE DIRECT GAP SITS
  WELL ABOVE THE BAND EDGE AND DOES NOT PRODUCE THE RED AND GREEN EMISSION.
- **NEW:** EMISSION IS DESCRIBED AS PROCEEDING THROUGH ISOELECTRONIC NITROGEN
  CENTRES, WHICH IS WHY GAP EMITS AT ALL DESPITE THE INDIRECT GAP.

### 3.5 RADIUS RATIO RULE
- **OLD:** ONE SIDED QUOTING SUCH AS "AT LEAST 0.414".
- **NEW:** TWO SIDED WINDOWS ARE STATED IN FULL. TETRAHEDRAL 0.225 TO 0.414,
  OCTAHEDRAL 0.414 TO 0.732, CUBIC ABOVE 0.732. THE NARRATION ALSO STATES THE
  EXCLUSIONS: AUZN IS METALLIC AND GAP IS COVALENT, SO NEITHER IS A VALID TEST
  CASE, AND IT REPORTS THE TWO GENUINE FAILURES, CEO2 AT 0.7029 (PREDICTS
  OCTAHEDRAL, OBSERVES CUBIC EIGHT COORDINATION, AN EDGE CASE) AND K2O AT 0.9648
  (THE RULE IS BUILT FOR 1:1 STOICHIOMETRY AND K2O IS 2:1).

### 3.6 NUMBERS THAT WERE MISSING
ADDED: CURIE TEMPERATURE 393 K; MEASURED SPONTANEOUS POLARISATION
0.26 COULOMB PER SQUARE METRE; REMANENCE 0.21; COERCIVE FIELD 0.5 MEGAVOLT PER
METRE; LITERATURE AXIAL RATIO NEAR 1.011 AGAINST THE BRIEF VALUE 1.0282; AND THE
SUM RULE THAT THE SHORT AND LONG TITANIUM OXYGEN BONDS ALWAYS ADD TO 410.27

### 8.1 WHAT THE NEW SCRIPT DOES PER SLIDE

70 Hz HIGHPASS, OPTIONAL FFT DENOISE (OFF BY DEFAULT), LEADING AND TRAILING
SILENCE TRIMMED AT -50 dB PEAK, **TWO PASS** EBU R128 TO I = -16 LUFS AND
TP = -1.5 dBTP, RESAMPLE TO 48 kHz STEREO s16, THEN 0.25 s OF LEAD IN AND 0.60 s
OF LEAD OUT. TWO PASS MATTERS: SINGLE PASS LOUDNORM IS DYNAMIC AND PUMPS THE
QUIET SLIDES. THE TAKES ARRIVED UNUSUALLY CONSISTENT, MEAN LEVEL -24.8 TO
-27.3 dB, AND CAME OUT BETWEEN -16.5 AND -17.1 LUFS ON FOURTEEN OF FIFTEEN.

### 8.2 CHECKS THAT ARE NOW FATAL

- A NUMBERED SLIDE WITH NO RECORDING
- A RECORDING THAT MATCHES NO SLIDE, WHICH IS AN ERROR AND NOT A SILENT SKIP
- TWO RECORDINGS CLAIMING THE SAME SLIDE
- A NON FINITE LOUDNORM MEASUREMENT, WHICH MEANS THE TAKE IS SILENT
- CONCATENATED AUDIO DISAGREEING WITH THE PLANNED TIMELINE BY MORE THAN 0.25 s
- A FINISHED MP4 DISAGREEING WITH THE PLANNED TIMELINE BY MORE THAN 0.5 s

A TAKE SHORTER THAN 0.35 OF THE MEDIAN IS A WARNING, PROMOTED TO FATAL BY
`CML_STRICT=1`. IT FIRED ON THE FIRST BUILD: THE SLIDE 15 TAKE IS 4.4 s AGAINST
A MEDIAN OF 96.4 s, SO THE VERIFICATION AND MANIFEST SLIDE IS EFFECTIVELY
UNNARRATED. THAT IS A KNOWN OPEN ITEM, NOT A PIPELINE FAULT.
