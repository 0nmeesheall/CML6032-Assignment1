## NOTATION

LENGTHS ARE IN pm THROUGHOUT (1 ANGSTROM = 100 pm). RATIOS ARE QUOTED AS RATIOS AND NEVER PER 100.
OXIDATION STATES ARE SINGLE TUPLES, FOR EXAMPLE `Ca(+2, SPIN UNASSIGNED)`. MIRROR PLANES ARE WRITTEN MP.
ROTOINVERSION AXES AND SPACE GROUPS CARRY A TRUE OVERHEAD BAR: m3̄m, Fm3̄m, Pm3̄m, F4̄3m, 6̄m2, 3̄m,
{101̄0}, {101̄1}, AND THE FULL SYMBOL 4/m 3̄ 2/m. TWO DELIBERATE EXCEPTIONS:

1. THE COMMENT HEADERS INSIDE THE CIF FILES KEEP THE ASCII HYPHEN FORM (`Fm-3m`). A CIF IS A MACHINE FILE
   READ BY VESTA AND BY OTHER CRYSTALLOGRAPHY TOOLS, AND A NON-ASCII BYTE IN A HEADER CAN STOP A FILE FROM
   OPENING AT ALL. THE COSMETIC GAIN IS NOT WORTH THAT RISK.
2. THE THREE QUESTION SLIDES QUOTE THE ISSUED BRIEF VERBATIM, SO THEY KEEP THE BRIEF'S OWN `a (Å)` COLUMN
   HEADING. EVERY ANSWER SLIDE CONVERTS IT TO pm.

THE SPOKEN NARRATION IS THE AUTHOR'S OWN VOICE AND SAYS UNITS AND BARS OUT IN FULL ("PICOMETRES",
"m THREE BAR m"), BECAUSE A BAR AND A HYPHEN SOUND THE SAME AND ONLY ONE OF THEM IS CORRECT.

## THE HYSTERESIS FIGURE

`figures/hysteresis_loop_*.png` IS DRAWN WITH TWO SEPARATE BRANCHES. THE DESCENDING BRANCH REACHES P = 0 AT
E = -Ec AND THE ASCENDING BRANCH REACHES P = 0 AT E = +Ec, SO THE CURVE IS DOUBLE VALUED AND NEVER PASSES
THROUGH THE ORIGIN. PLOTTED FROM Ps = 0.26 C m-2, Pr = 0.21 C m-2 AND Ec = 0.5 MV m-1 WITH A TANH SWITCHING
MODEL; IT IS A LABELLED SCHEMATIC AND NOT MEASURED DATA. THE RIGID-ION Ti-ONLY SUM USED IN PART B SEGMENT 1
GIVES ONLY 0.0805 C m-2 AT THE EXTREME STATE, WHICH IS WHY THE MEASURED Ps SETS THE LOOP HEIGHT.

## DELIBERATE CHOICES AND LIMITATIONS

1. THE SUPPLIED CELL GIVES LP3/LP1 = 1.028, ABOVE THE LITERATURE ROOM-TEMPERATURE VALUE OF ABOUT 1.011. CELL
   EDITS WERE NOT MADE BECAUSE THE BRIEF RESTRICTS CHANGES TO THE Ti COORDINATE, SO THE SERIES IS QUALITATIVE.
2. ONLY Ti MOVES. IN THE REAL STRUCTURE THE OXYGEN SUBLATTICE MOVES OPPOSITE TO Ti, SO THE TABULATED P VALUES
   ARE LOWER BOUNDS ON THE MEASURED Ps OF ABOUT 0.26 C m^-2.
3. REMANENCE AND COERCIVITY ARE DOMAIN PROPERTIES. AT E = PLUS OR MINUS Ec A REAL CRYSTAL IS A 50:50 MIXTURE OF
   PLUS-c AND MINUS-c DOMAINS WITH ZERO NET P; IT IS NOT A LATTICE OF CENTROSYMMETRIC CELLS.
4. PART A ASSIGNS THE MOST PROBABLE POINT GROUP. EXTERNAL MORPHOLOGY FIXES THE CLASS ONLY UP TO THE HOLOHEDRY
   OF THE LATTICE AND CANNOT PROVE CENTROSYMMETRY.

## ION RADII IN VESTA

EVERY CIF CARRIES `_atom_type_oxidation_number`. THIS MATTERS: WITHOUT A FORMAL
CHARGE VESTA CANNOT RESOLVE AN IONIC RADIUS AND SILENTLY FALLS BACK TO NEUTRAL
ATOMIC RADII, WHICH DRAWS Na LARGER THAN Cl AND Ti LARGER THAN O - THE REVERSE OF
THE IONIC PICTURE THE RADIUS-RATIO ARGUMENT DEPENDS ON. `patch_cif_charges.py`
APPLIES AND DOCUMENTS THE SCHEME.

AuZn AND GaP ARE LEFT AT ZERO CHARGE ON PURPOSE. AuZn IS METALLIC AND GaP IS
COVALENT, SO THE METALLIC AND COVALENT RADII VESTA USES AT ZERO CHARGE ARE THE
CORRECT ONES, AND NEITHER PHASE FALLS UNDER AN IONIC RADIUS-RATIO WINDOW.

AFTER OPENING A CIF, SET EDIT > PREFERENCES > ATOMS TO IONIC RADII.

## ANNOTATED BaTiO3 FRAMES

`renders/batio3/` HOLDS THE FIVE Ti DISPLACEMENT STATES AT 1920 BY 1080 WITH THE
BOND NUMBERS PRINTED ON EACH FRAME. THE BARE VESTA RENDERS ARE UNUSABLE ON THEIR
OWN: ACROSS THE WHOLE SCAN LESS THAN 0.01 OF THE PIXELS CHANGE, BECAUSE Ti MOVES
ABOUT 14 px WHILE BEING DRAWN ABOUT 96 px WIDE. EACH ANNOTATED FRAME THEREFORE
CARRIES A DASHED z = 0.500 REFERENCE LINE, A SOLID LINE AT THE MEASURED Ti CENTRE,
AND A DETAIL AT 2.5 TIMES SCALE WITH THE OFFSET BRACKETED IN pm.

`renders/batio3/annotate_batio3.py` RE-MEASURES THE Ti POSITION FROM THE IMAGES,
SO IT CAN BE RE-RUN ON FRESHLY RE-RENDERED FRAMES AFTER THE CHARGE PATCH.
