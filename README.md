ABOUT 2:1 LENGTHWAYS. 222 AND mm2 GROW THE SAME BOX BUT WOULD SHOW HALF-FACES OR A POLAR END. NONE ARE
RESOLVED, SO THE HOLOHEDRY IS TAKEN: **mmm (D2h, CLASS 8)**, FULL SYMBOL 2/m 2/m 2/m, WITH 8 OPERATIONS:
E, THREE 2-FOLD AXES FACE TO FACE, THREE MP EACH PARALLEL TO ONE FACE PAIR, AND A CENTRE i. IF CALIPERS
FOUND A SQUARE CROSS-SECTION ON EVERY CRYSTAL, THE ANSWER WOULD RISE TO 4/mmm.

DECISIVE CHECK, NOT YET RUN: LAY A CLEAR CRYSTAL BETWEEN CROSSED POLARISERS (A WHITE LCD SCREEN BELOW, A
POLARISING FILTER ABOVE) AND TURN IT, ONCE ON EACH OF ITS THREE FACE TYPES. AN ORTHORHOMBIC (mmm) CRYSTAL
GOES DARK EVERY 90 DEGREES ON ALL THREE AND BRIGHTENS IN BETWEEN, BUT IS NEVER DARK THROUGH A FULL TURN. A
TETRAGONAL ONE STAYS DARK THROUGH A FULL TURN ONLY WHEN VIEWED DOWN ITS UNIQUE AXIS. A CUBIC ONE STAYS DARK
ON ALL THREE.

## ION RADII IN VESTA

EVERY CIF CARRIES `_atom_type_oxidation_number`. THIS MATTERS: WITHOUT A FORMAL
CHARGE VESTA CANNOT RESOLVE AN IONIC RADIUS AND SILENTLY FALLS BACK TO NEUTRAL
ATOMIC RADII, WHICH DRAWS Na LARGER THAN Cl AND Ti LARGER THAN O - THE REVERSE OF
THE IONIC PICTURE THE RADIUS-RATIO ARGUMENT DEPENDS ON. `patch_cif_charges.py`
APPLIES AND DOCUMENTS THE SCHEME.

AuZn AND GaP ARE LEFT AT ZERO CHARGE ON PURPOSE. AuZn IS METALLIC AND GaP IS
COVALENT, SO THE METALLIC AND COVALENT RADII VESTA USES AT ZERO CHARGE ARE THE
CORRECT ONES, AND NEITHER PHASE FALLS UNDER AN IONIC RADIUS-RATIO WINDOW.

AFTER OPENING A CIF, SET EDIT > PREFERENCES > ATOMS TO IONIC RADII.
